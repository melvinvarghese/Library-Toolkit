Flexible shelf labels for DDC, UDC and local schemes

Enter classification numbers exactly as assigned by your library. The generator treats call numbers as display text, so it does not impose numbering rules on DDC, UDC, or local classification systems. Import a CSV, review the sheet and print at your selected label dimensions.
